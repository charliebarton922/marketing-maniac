Brand Name: Volt Rush

Brand Voice:
Energetic
College-focused
Confident
Fun but not reckless
Modern and clean
Not overly corporate
Not too slang-heavy
Social and upbeat
Clear and direct
Bold but still believable
Trendy but not cringey
Friendly and engaging

Tone Rules:
Sound exciting and social
Keep wording clear and natural
Avoid sounding fake or exaggerated
Avoid sounding too aggressive
Avoid sounding like a medical or scientific product
Avoid sounding overly salesy
Avoid too many exclamation points
Avoid too much hype in one sentence
Keep it polished enough for a real brand
Keep it relatable to students and young adults
Use light, natural energy in the writing
Do not sound childish
Do not sound reckless, extreme, or irresponsible

Style Preferences:
Short to medium-length sentences
Easy to read quickly
Strong hooks
Natural calls to action
Moderate emoji use only when it fits
Use hashtags only when appropriate for the platform
